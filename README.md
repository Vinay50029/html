A website dedicated to showcasing the BMW M4 EVO, offering essential details about BMW and its M series. Features include:
    Detailed car views and part descriptions.
    A short promotional video of the vehicle.
    Integrated maps for showroom visits.
    Creator information and links to BMW's social media.
Test Drive Section:
    Navigation bar and a video of the BMW driving experience.
    Test Drive Booking System: Users can fill out a form and receive an automated email confirmation of their booking.
